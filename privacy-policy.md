# Privacy Policy for AfterWorks Chat App

Effective Date: [April 28, 2025]

Thank you for using AfterWorks, a simple anonymous chat application.

## 1. What Information We Collect
We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII). Users are required to enter a temporary nickname which is not linked to any real-world identity.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The temporary nickname and chat messages are used only during the chat session. All messages are deleted when users leave the session or become inactive.

## 3. Data Storage and Deletion
Messages are stored in a secure Firebase Realtime Database during the session and automatically deleted when the chat ends. No chat history is retained.

## 4. Security
We use Firebase, which is compliant with industry-standard security measures to protect temporary data during transmission.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Our app is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

## 7. Contact
If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us at: **afterhoursofworks@gmail.com**
